Who I Am

Hugues Duproz

Tech professional by day, cheese enthusiast always.

For years, I've been exploring cheesemongers and meeting producers to discover the finest cheeses in Europe. My passion for terroir extends to wine and beer — a natural pairing that gives rise to the most delicious combinations.

Trained at the École Française du Fromage (French School of Cheese), specialized in sensory analysis (identifying flavors and aromas) and tasting facilitation, I started sharing my passion with friends, then colleagues, then groups of individuals, and today with companies.
